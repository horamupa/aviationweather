Privacy Policy

Aviation Weather for iPhone and iPad
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Aviation Weather app ("the App", "we") handles your information. The short version: the App has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email, location or any other personal details.

Information we collect

None. The App contains no analytics or advertising code and never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We run no server that receives data from the App.

Data stored on your device

Your aircraft profiles, routes, logbook entries and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes them. A logbook export leaves the device only when you share the file yourself.

Location

If you allow it, the App uses your location while it is open to list the nearest airports and to show where you are on the map. Your location is used on the device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Weather requests

To show weather, the App downloads data from public weather services. These requests contain what the data needs: airport codes, the coordinates of points along your route or of a point you tap on the map, and the map area on screen. They do not contain your name, your device location or any identifier of yours. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to the service that answers. The services are:

  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov) and other NOAA services: reports, forecasts, hazards and radar;
  • Open-Meteo: model winds, temperatures and surface forecasts. Open-Meteo terms and privacy;
  • NASA GIBS, EUMETSAT and DWD: satellite and radar images;
  • ga-tiles.aviationweather.pro, our map tiles on Google Firebase Hosting, which serves the same files to everyone. Firebase privacy.

Links to official pages (FAA, Flight Service and others) open only when you tap them.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
